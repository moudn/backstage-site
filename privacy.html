3. Visiting this website

Server logs

Our host, Cloudflare, records ordinary web-server information for every request: your IP address, which browser and operating system you are using, which page you asked for, and when. We do not use this to build a picture of you as an individual, and we do not combine it with anything else.

  • Why: to serve the site, and to absorb attacks and abuse.
  • Lawful basis: legitimate interests — running a website securely. Nobody can operate a site without their server knowing who asked it for a page, and the intrusion is minimal.

Your light/dark preference

If you press the light/dark toggle, your browser stores that choice locally under the name backstage-theme. It records one word, light or dark. It is never transmitted to us and never leaves your device. Clearing your browser's site data removes it. If you never press the toggle, nothing is stored at all and the site simply follows your operating system's own setting.

Cookies, and why there is no banner

There are none. No first-party cookies, no third-party cookies, no analytics, no advertising or social pixels, no session tracking, no fingerprinting.

You have not been asked to accept anything because there is nothing to accept. Under the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations, consent is required for storage that is not necessary for a service you asked for. The only storage here is the preference you set yourself by pressing a button — which is exactly the sort of functional storage the rules exempt. If we ever add analytics, this notice changes first and you will be asked properly.

Two things follow from this that are worth stating, because most sites cannot say them. The typefaces are served from our own domain rather than Google Fonts, so Google is not told your IP address when the page loads. And nothing else on the page calls out to another company at all.

Reply with the word "stop", or email {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}, and that is the end of it. No form, no reason required, no "are you sure". We keep the minimum record needed to make sure we do not contact you again by mistake — which is the one thing suppressing you actually requires — and delete the rest.
